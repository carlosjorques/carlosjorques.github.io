## How I Got Here

My curiosity for electronics led me to build my first **obstacle-avoiding robot kit**.

The real turning point happened at **Campus Party in Valencia**, where I watched a line-tracking robot competition. Some robots were incredibly fast and stable, while mine oscillated from side to side. I wanted to understand why.

During my **Electrical Engineering** studies, I found one answer in a **PI controller**. That was my first clear step into control engineering. It gave me a language for many things I had wondered about, from balancing a Segway to how climate control regulates a room.

I started my **Industrial Engineering** studies in Valencia and later continued in Sweden through a double-degree program in **Electrical Engineering**, including advanced control studies at **Lund University**. There, I deepened my focus on automatic control, dynamic systems, and model-based engineering. That path eventually led me to pursue a **PhD** and apply those ideas to industrial control problems.

Today, I develop control software for vehicles and intelligent machines, providing technical guidance across architecture, validation, and delivery so the systems we build can operate reliably in the real world.

> "As a child, I used to take toys apart to see what was inside because I wanted to become an inventor"

## How I Work

The real engineering challenge is turning control ideas into **real-time software** that can be integrated, calibrated, and maintained across a **long field lifecycle**. I value **simple, robust solutions** over unnecessary complexity, because advanced algorithms only matter when they respect **hardware constraints, safety-related requirements, and validation evidence**, and the realities of production delivery.

When solving technical problems, I actively question underlying assumptions, verify them with **empirical data**, and separate **root cause, control strategy, diagnostics, and degradation logic** before jumping into code.

As a leader, my role is to bring **structural clarity**, remove friction, and provide the team with the practical context and tools they need to execute with autonomy and accountability. I guide deliveries by making technical trade-offs visible, managing risk together, and turning different engineering perspectives into sound technical decisions.

> "An elegant algorithm is only the beginning."

## Technical Areas & Capabilities

The core engineering areas where my work connects control design, real-time software, diagnostics, system validation, and technical team direction.

### Controls Architecture

Challenge: Define behavior before implementation

Model-based control architecture for feedback control, predictive strategies, optimal actuation, state estimation, and virtual sensing, applied to torque control, thermal management, and BMS systems.

### Real-Time Embedded Software

Challenge: Translate control logic into production code

Development and integration of real-time embedded application software using MATLAB/Simulink, C/C++, AUTOSAR workflows, automated CI/CD pipelines, and standard versioning practices.

### Diagnostics & Fault Isolation

Challenge: Make physical system health observable

Online diagnostics for fault detection, isolation, and automated degradation strategies that improve safety, system availability, and controlled behavior under component failures.

### Validation, MIL/SIL/HIL, and Field Evidence

Challenge: Connect functional requirements with empirical data

Verification of embedded control software against specifications through MIL/SIL/HIL environments, lab testing, wind-tunnel testing, vehicle logs, and field data analysis.

### System Integration

Challenge: Keep software aligned with the physical machine

Integration of software layers with high-voltage batteries, chargers, actuators, and sensors over CAN networks, ensuring calibrated behavior under real operating loads.

### Technical Leadership

Challenge: Turn technical ambiguity into shared direction

Technical review, mentoring, and team direction across the software development lifecycle to maintain system coherence, support decision-making, and reach release milestones.
