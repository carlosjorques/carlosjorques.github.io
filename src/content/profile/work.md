### Intro

Selected work, applied domains, and research behind my work in embedded controls for complex physical systems.

I develop real-time control software for systems where physical behavior, timing, diagnostics, validation, and production constraints must come together. This page brings together selected engineering contributions, the domains I work in, and the research, patents, and publications that support my approach to reliable embedded control software.

## Applied Domains

### Heavy-Duty Transport & Electrification

Context: Scania trucks and powertrain control systems

Real-time control software for internal combustion and electric heavy-duty truck platforms, including thermal management, high-voltage battery systems, charger coordination, and powertrain control.

### EV Thermal Management

Context: Battery systems, charging, auxiliaries, and component protection

Control architecture, model design, diagnostics, and calibration strategies for thermal systems where performance affects efficiency, charging behavior, component protection, and battery lifespan.

### Autonomous Mobility Systems

Context: Volvo CE autonomous electric haulers

Propulsion and vehicle-control software for autonomous electric machines, including traction and speed control, torque allocation, safety monitoring, and vehicle-level behavior.

### Safety-Related Diagnostics

Context: Fault isolation, degradation logic, and controlled behavior under faults

Real-time monitoring software to detect faults, isolate component failures, and trigger degradation modes so vehicles and machines can remain controlled under fault conditions.

### Power Electronics and Electric Mobility

Context: Mahle electrified powertrain and power electronics environments

Model-based thermal and control software for e-bike motor control, wireless charging, DC/DC converter interfaces, power electronics, and AUTOSAR-oriented software architecture.
